Questions

How do I convert a file?
  1. Drag a file in Explorer or on the desktop. Keep the left mouse button held.
  2. Tap Ctrl. The wheel of formats opens.
  3. Drop the file on the format you want.

Changed your mind? Press Esc to close the wheel.

Can I convert several files at once?

Yes. Select the files, then drag and tap Ctrl as usual.

The wheel shows the formats that every selected file can become.

Where do converted files go?

Right next to the original, in the same folder. The original is never changed.

Prefer one place for everything? Choose it under Save conversions to in Settings.

Can I use the right-click menu instead?
  1. Right-click a file, then Show more options on Windows 11.
  2. Choose Convert with Pomelo, then click a format.
Does Pomelo upload my files?

No. Everything happens on this PC.

Pomelo goes online only to check for updates or to add an engine, and only when you ask it to.

A format I need is missing

Some conversions need an extra engine, such as the PDF components or LibreOffice.

  1. Open Settings and press Advanced.
  2. Under Conversion engines, press Install next to the one you need.
Where is Pomelo?

Pomelo lives in the tray, by the clock. Windows often tucks it behind the little arrow. Click its icon to open Settings.
