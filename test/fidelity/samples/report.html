3. Outlook

We expect growth to moderate to the low double digits as the installed base matures, while the data residency controls open two regulated sectors that were previously out of reach. Capital expenditure will rise by roughly a fifth to fund the campus and the second data-centre region, and the board has approved a share buy-back of up to RM 12 million, to be executed opportunistically.

Risks remain: currency volatility, longer sales cycles in the public sector, and competition for senior engineering talent. Mitigations are set out in the risk register, which management reviews monthly.
