Wake up above the clouds

Each morning begins with a pot of fresh tea on the veranda while the valley fills with mist. Our guides lead gentle hikes through mossy forest and working plantations, and every afternoon is left open for rest.

Evenings are for long dinners built around the region’s produce: strawberries, mountain herbs, and the famous steamboat. Rooms are warm, quiet, and look out over the terraces.

What is included

  • Three nights in a boutique lodge
  • Daily breakfast and two dinners
  • Guided plantation and forest walks
  • Return transfers from Kuala Lumpur

Prefer a different pace? Every day has an easy option and a challenge option, and you can swap at breakfast.

Good to know

Pack layers: evenings fall to 14°C. Trails are mostly paved, with one steeper section on day three. We cater for vegetarian, vegan and halal diets with notice.
