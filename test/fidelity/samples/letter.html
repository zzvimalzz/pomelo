Dear Members of the Committee,

It is my great pleasure to recommend Mr. Ahmad Zulkifli for the Merdeka Research Fellowship. I have supervised his work for three years, first as his final-year project advisor and then as principal investigator on the sensor-networks project he joined as a research assistant. In that time he has shown the rare combination of rigour, curiosity and generosity that marks a future leader in the field.

Research record

Ahmad’s project on low-power mesh networking produced a protocol that cut energy use by 37% in field trials, and he presented the results at two international conferences. Notable contributions include:

  • designing the adaptive duty-cycling scheme at the core of the protocol;
  • building the open test-bed now used by four other groups in the faculty;
  • mentoring six undergraduate students through their capstone projects.
