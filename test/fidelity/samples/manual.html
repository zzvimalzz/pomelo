1. Getting started

Beacon Router is a dual-band wireless router designed for small offices. This guide explains how to install, configure and maintain it. Read the safety notice in the box before powering the device.

Note. The default administrator address is 192.168.8.1. Change the password at first sign-in.

1.1 Connecting the hardware

  1. Attach both antennas and place the router away from metal surfaces.
  2. Connect the WAN port to your modem with the yellow cable.
  3. Connect the power adapter and wait until the status light turns steady green.
  4. Join the network named Beacon-Setup from your computer.

1.2 Signing in

Open a browser and go to http://192.168.8.1. Enter the default user name admin and the password printed on the label. Press Enter to continue. If the page does not load, see support.example.com/beacon.

3. Security

Enable WPA3 where all your devices support it; otherwise choose WPA2/WPA3 mixed mode. Turn off WPS once every device is connected, and enable automatic firmware updates so security fixes arrive without delay.

Warning. Never expose the administration page to the internet. Remote management should be used only through the built-in VPN.

3.1 Guest network

  • Isolates guests from your devices.
  • Limits bandwidth to protect your own traffic.
    • Default limit: 20 Mbit/s per guest.
  • Expires automatically after 24 hours.
